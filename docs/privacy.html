Privacy Policy

App: Magnet  ·  Last updated: June 9, 2026  ·  Effective: June 9, 2026

1. Overview

Magnet ("we", "our", "the app") analyzes Instagram profile screenshots as creative works to provide personalized presentation feedback. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Photos you upload: You provide up to 3 Instagram profile screenshots per analysis session. These images are sent to our analysis server and to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate your report. Images are not stored permanently — they are held in temporary server memory for the duration of your session only and are discarded when the session ends or the server restarts.
  • Purchase data: Credit purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect, store, or see your payment information. We receive only a confirmation of successful purchase to credit your account balance.
  • Usage preferences: Your remaining credit balance and selected preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard device storage (AsyncStorage / Keychain). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • No account required: Magnet does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate your profile analysis report (images sent to Claude API during active session only).
  • To process and verify in-app purchases via Apple StoreKit.
  • To maintain your credit balance on your device.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API: Profile images and analysis prompts are sent to Anthropic's API to generate analysis text. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: anthropic.com/privacy. Per Anthropic's API terms, data submitted via the API is not used to train models.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Handles all in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data Retention

Uploaded photos exist only in volatile server memory during your active session (typically minutes). No photos are written to disk or backed up. Session data is lost when the server restarts. Your device-local credit balance persists until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

6. Children's Privacy

Magnet is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 17. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you wish to remove locally stored data, uninstall the app from your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us: yezhonglin2015@gmail.com

隐私政策

应用：展示面磁力（Magnet） ·  最后更新：2026年6月9日  ·  生效日期：2026年6月9日

1. 概述

展示面磁力（以下简称"本应用"）将 Instagram 主页截图作为创作作品进行分析，为您提供个性化的展示面建议。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们收集哪些数据、如何使用，以及您享有的权利。

2. 我们收集的数据

  • 您上传的照片：每次分析您最多提供 3 张 Instagram 主页截图。这些图片仅用于生成分析报告，会被发送至我们的分析服务器和 Anthropic 的 Claude API。图片不会被永久存储——仅在会话期间保存于服务器临时内存中，会话结束或服务器重启后即被丢弃。
  • 购买数据：次数购买完全由 Apple App Store（StoreKit）处理。我们不收集、存储或查看您的支付信息，仅接收购买成功的确认通知以增加您的次数余额。
  • 使用偏好：您的次数余额和选择偏好仅存储在您的设备本地（AsyncStorage / Keychain），不会传输到我们的服务器。
  • 无需注册账户：本应用无需创建账户，我们不收集您的姓名、电子邮件、手机号码或任何可识别个人身份的信息。

3. 数据的使用方式

  • 生成您的展示面分析报告（图片仅在活跃会话期间发送至 Claude API）。
  • 通过 Apple StoreKit 处理和验证应用内购买。
  • 在您的设备上维护次数余额。

我们不将您的数据用于广告、用户画像或任何与提供服务无关的目的。

4. 第三方服务

  • Anthropic Claude API：主页图片和分析提示词会发送至 Anthropic API 以生成分析文字。Anthropic 的隐私政策适用：anthropic.com/privacy。根据 Anthropic API 条款，通过 API 提交的数据不用于训练模型。
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：处理所有应用内购买。Apple 的隐私政策适用：apple.com/legal/privacy。

5. 数据保留

上传的照片仅存在于会话期间的服务器临时内存中（通常仅几分钟），不写入磁盘，不做备份。服务器重启后会话数据即消失。设备本地的次数余额在卸载应用或清除应用数据前持续保留。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用适合 17 岁及以上用户使用。我们不会故意收集 17 岁以下用户的任何数据。如您认为有未成年人使用了本应用，请联系我们。

7. 您的权利

由于我们不在服务器上存储个人数据，没有需要代您检索、更正或删除的内容。如需删除本地存储的数据，请在设备上卸载本应用。

8. 政策变更

随应用发展，我们可能更新本政策。页面顶部的"最后更新"日期将反映任何变更。在变更后继续使用本应用即视为接受更新后的政策。
